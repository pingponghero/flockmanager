## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Flock Manager is the complete companion app for backyard chicken keepers. Whether you have 3 hens or 30, keep your flock organized and track everything that matters.

**EGG TRACKING**
- Log daily egg collection with one tap
- Track eggs by bird (with color-coded tags)
- View production trends with beautiful charts
- See weekly and monthly statistics at a glance

**FLOCK MANAGEMENT**
- Create multiple flocks (layers, meat birds, etc.)
- Add individual birds with photos, breeds, and hatch dates
- Track bird status (active, sold, deceased)
- Breed suggestions from 50+ chicken breeds

**HEALTH & MEDICATIONS**
- Log treatments and vaccinations
- Track medication withdrawal periods
- Get warnings when eggs are unsafe to consume
- Reference guide for common chicken medications

**EXPENSE TRACKING**
- Track feed, supplies, and equipment costs
- Log income from egg sales
- Calculate cost-per-egg and profit/loss
- View expense breakdowns by category

**BREED REFERENCE GUIDE**
- 50 popular chicken breeds
- Filter by egg color, temperament, and climate hardiness
- Detailed info: egg production, weight, characteristics
- Perfect for choosing your next breed

**BEAUTIFUL & CUSTOMIZABLE**
- Clean, modern design
- Three color themes to match your style
- Works offline - no internet required
- Your data stays on your device

Flock Manager was designed by chicken keepers, for chicken keepers. We understand that your birds are part of the family, and managing your flock should be simple and enjoyable.

Download now and take the guesswork out of backyard chicken keeping!
