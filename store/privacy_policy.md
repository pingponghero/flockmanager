# Flock Manager Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

## Overview

Flock Manager ("the App") is a local-first application for tracking backyard chicken flocks. Your privacy is important to us, and we've designed Flock Manager to keep your data under your control.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect
**Nothing.** Flock Manager does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

### What Stays on Your Device
All data you enter in Flock Manager is stored locally on your device:
- Flock and bird information
- Egg production records
- Expense and income records
- Medication and health records
- Photos you add to bird profiles
- App preferences and theme settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or back it up.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Photos are stored in the app's private storage directory.

## Third-Party Services

Flock Manager does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

- **Camera/Photos:** Only used when you choose to add photos to bird profiles. Photos are stored locally and never uploaded.

## Data Backup

Device backups (iCloud, Google Backup) may include Flock Manager data as part of your device's standard backup process. This is handled by your device's operating system, not by the App.

## Children's Privacy

Flock Manager does not collect any personal information and is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App and on our website.
